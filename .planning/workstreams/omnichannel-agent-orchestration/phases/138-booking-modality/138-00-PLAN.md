<objective>
Give an organization a business type that an operator sets in the panel, and derive the
booking modality from it, so the next tenant is configured by answering what kind of
business it is rather than by someone writing SQL.
</objective>

<context>
The operator's requirement, in their words: a cleaning company needs the customer's
address because it travels to them; a barbershop does not, because the customer comes to
the shop. That difference has to be adaptable per business, and it belongs in the panel.

Verified before writing this plan: **no such field exists anywhere.** `organizations`
carries `legal_name`, `tax_id`, address, `timezone`, `default_currency` and a `settings`
JSON — and no industry or business type. The `industry` column that does exist belongs to
`org_templates` (the sector a template serves) and to `accounts` (companies in the
tenant's own CRM); neither describes the tenant. Nothing in `organizations.settings`
stores a modality either.

`Settings → Company Info` already edits exactly this class of fact — legal identity,
address, timezone, currency — through `company-info/actions.ts`, so it is where this
belongs and no new page is needed.

Without this plan, `service_location_mode` would ship as a column nobody can set without
database access, which is the same defect the operator objected to in Phase 139's context.
</context>

The mapping is a default, not a law: a barbershop that does home visits must be able to override it, so 138-01's `service_location_mode` column stays the authority and this only supplies its initial value. Unknown or missing business type must fall back to the on-premises mode, never to one that asks for an address.</action>
